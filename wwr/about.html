Wild Water Rafting

Our mission is simple: get you out on the river, keep you safe, and send you home with a story worth telling. Every guide on our team lives for the rapids and loves sharing them.

History

Wild Water Rafting began in 2008 with one battered raft, two guides and a lot of enthusiasm. Today we run trips on three rivers and have guided thousands of happy adventurers.
